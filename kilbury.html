Kilbury

Royalty, agriculture, and journey map.

First Impressions

Getting into the entrance of the Kilbury Tour is kind of hidden, and hard to see. They have "secret" paths through the forest to get there and its a gated community.

Things I Noticed

  • The amount of land acres belonged to the Kilbury Family
  • The family still lives in the castle
  • The house has been used for movies, events, weddings.

    • What I Recommend

    Similar to my travel to Malahide, this is a castle and garden journey that I recommend to anyone into historical shows, history, architecture. There is a student discount, and there are plenty of things to do outside of the paid experience. Theres a pizza shop, cafe, farmers shop, once you get into the entrance all in the same area.
