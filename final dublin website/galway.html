Galway

Colorful streets, live music, and rainy walks.

First Impressions

Galway felt very lively and artistic compared to other places I visited. The streets were crowded with music, small shops, and people walking around late at night.

Food Review

I tried fish and chips near the city center and it was one of my favorite meals from the trip.

Things I Noticed

  • A lot of live street performers
  • Very walkable city
  • Colorful buildings and shops

What I Recommend

I recommend exploring the smaller streets instead of only the tourist areas.
