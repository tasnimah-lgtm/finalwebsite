Howth

Coastal scenary, blue sea and hiking.

First Impressions

Howth felt very warm and breezy, its surrounded by the beach and has a bunch of nature activities.

Food Review

I tried ice cream there, and it was delicious! They also have a food market on the weekends, which is nice to try out.

Things I Noticed

  • Architecture of neighborhoods
  • Tourist attraction
  • Lighthouses

What I Recommend

I recommend hiking up the cliff, and doing the whole thing on a day you are free.
