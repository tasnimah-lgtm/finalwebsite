Malahide

Butterflys, Fairy Gardens, and Castles.

First Impressions

Malahide is a beautiful scenic place to visit with lots to do.

Things I Noticed

  • Small community market
  • City is different from tourist attractions
  • Historical backgrounds tied to buildings

  • What I Recommend

    There are local businesses and markets you can visit that often serve crepes, home-made skin care & creams, pita, etc. If you are interested in castle and gardens at all, the tour is something you should look into. If you are a student, its $12 for the entire tour, which takes about 2-3 hours to complete. The fairy trail is one of the longer parts to complete, with numbers ranked in different areas to follow and mythology stories along the way.
